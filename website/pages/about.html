Company

From a university project to a company

NEST started as a reading list and an architecture sketch at Istanbul Commerce University in February 2026. Seven months later it is a working codebase, one withdrawn number, and a decision to incorporate. Here is the mission, the timeline, the people and how to reach us.

Mission

Two things worth building

Give people who cannot speak or type a non-invasive path to open text: no surgery, no fixed phrase set, a decoding layer that runs on any research-grade EEG system. And give the field a benchmark it can trust, in an area where published scores have repeatedly turned out to reward the wrong thing once someone checked them properly, including a number NEST itself had to withdraw.

Principles

What we will not compromise on

Measure before claiming

A February 2026 estimate had to be withdrawn once the team compared it against real decoding, so nothing ships now until it has been decoded, not inferred from a loss curve.

Open by default

Code, data loaders and evaluation scripts are public, so any claim on this site can be checked instead of taken on faith.

Hardware-agnostic

NEST is a software layer, not a headset, so any research-grade EEG system that can output word-aligned frequency-band features is a valid input.

Research tool first, clinical product later

Making no diagnostic or therapeutic claim keeps NEST outside FDA, EU MDR and Turkish TITCK device scope today, and a future assistive product would take the CE/MDR path deliberately, not by accident.
